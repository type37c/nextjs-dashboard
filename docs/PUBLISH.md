# Public repository

Oscillator Mind is published at https://github.com/type37c/oscillator-mind. Its research website is https://basin-mv.com/. The repository was previously named `nextjs-dashboard`.

The previous Next.js tutorial is preserved in branch `archive/nextjs-tutorial-2026-11-05`, commit `a294ceb1cff6ff1f910c759760e6fa5df1a60b66`. The research release is a normal descendant commit; existing history was not rewritten.

The old Next.js tutorial and Vercel homepage belong to the archived project. The research textbook is hosted at basin-mv.com.

The working position paper is available under `papers/from_having_memory_to_using_it/`. Its publication notes distinguish the manuscript and included evidence snapshots from complete experimental reproduction packages.
